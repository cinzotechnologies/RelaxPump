Mains box (not on this PCB)

  • MCB → contactor (sized to the pump) → overload relay (≥ 1.5 HP) → pump.
  • 12 V DIN-rail interface relay: coil from PUMP; contact switches the 230 V contactor coil. RC snubber on the coil.
  • Existing starter panel: use the interface relay contact as a dry contact in its start circuit.
  • Electrician only. Earth everything metal.

Layout rules

  • 2-layer, 1.6 mm, 1 oz copper (2 oz if possible).
  • 12 V rail traces ≥ 2.5 mm (6 A); each valve output ≥ 1 mm (2 A).
  • No copper under the ESP32 antenna; antenna at the board edge.
  • Ground pour on both layers, stitched with vias.
  • Board 160 × 100 mm to fit an IP65 box; connectors on the edges.
